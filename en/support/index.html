Elowa: support

For questions, ideas, bug reports, or requests about your subscription or your data, write to support@elowaapp.com.

  • Subscription and cancelling: your subscription is managed and cancelled in your Google Play or App Store account. To bring a purchase to a new phone, use "Restore purchases" in the app.
  • Your data: in the app, go to Profile › Privacy to download it, withdraw your consent or delete it. More: Delete your data, Privacy policy.
  • Objecting to your personal plan: your plan is prepared automatically from your answers. If you want a person to look at it, write to this address.

Elowa is not an emergency service and this inbox is not watched around the clock. If you are in a hard moment, open Hard moment in the app or call your local emergency number.

Elowa is a self-care app. It is not a substitute for therapy, medical diagnosis or treatment. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number.
