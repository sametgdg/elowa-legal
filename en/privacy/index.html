Elowa Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective: 26 October 2026 · This policy applies to the app and to elowaapp.com.

In short

  • What you write and your emotional state are sensitive data; we process them only to be there for you, and only with your permission.
  • Your data is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). We don't sell it and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Your safety plan and the people you trust stay only on your phone.
  • You can withdraw your consent, download your data or delete it completely whenever you want.
  • The coach is an AI, not a person. Elowa is not therapy or medical treatment.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is the Elowa developer (an individual developer based in Türkiye). Contact: support@elowaapp.com. EU representative: not appointed yet.

2. What data we process, and why

The detailed list, purposes and legal grounds are in the Privacy Notice. In short:

  • Account and profile: to run the app (contract).
  • Data about your emotional state (topics, answers, mood, what you write, coach chat): for your personal plan, rituals and coach; only with your explicit consent.
  • Crisis screening: to show you support lines if crisis wording is noticed in what you write; under the same consent. No one is notified automatically.
  • Subscription: to unlock paid features (contract). Apple or Google takes the payment.
  • Technical logs: for security and debugging (legitimate interest).
  • Analytics, product improvement and e-mail: each with a separate, optional permission.

3. AI and your personal plan

  • The coach's replies are produced by an AI model. We always show this on the chat screen. The coach can make mistakes; don't base important decisions on it alone.
  • What you write is not used to train the model unless you give separate permission; if you do, it is de-identified first.
  • Your plan is prepared automatically from your answers. To object or ask a person to look at it: support@elowaapp.com.

4. Permissions on your phone

  • Notifications: for reminders; you can turn them off if you like. Your topic, feelings or concerns don't appear on the lock screen.
  • Microphone: only when you tap "write by speaking". Your voice is turned into text on your phone; it is not recorded and does not reach us.
  • We don't ask for access to your contacts. You type in the people you trust yourself; they stay on your phone. To call or message them, your phone's own app opens.
  • Phone lock (fingerprint, face): for the app lock; biometric data doesn't reach us, your phone does the check.
  • Advertising ID: we don't collect it. That's why we don't ask for tracking permission (ATT) on iOS.

5. Who we share it with

Only with the processors we work with to provide the service (Supabase, Render, Google Firebase, RevenueCat for subscriptions, an AI provider for the coach (not chosen yet)) and with the app stores (Apple, Google). The list and countries are in Privacy Notice §5. With authorized authorities where the law requires it. We don't share it with anyone else.

6. Transfers abroad

Our servers are in the EU. Some providers are US companies; Firebase and RevenueCat data may be processed in the US. For transfers from Türkiye we use the standard contracts under KVKK Art. 9; for transfers from the EU, the standard contractual clauses under the GDPR.

7. Security

Connections are encrypted (TLS). Each user can access only their own data. Sensitive data on your phone is encrypted with the phone's secure key store. There are options for an app lock and for hiding the screen in the background. Access is given only to the people who need it, with two-step verification. If there is a breach, we notify the competent authority within the legal time limit (72 hours) and you without delay.

8. Retention

As long as you use the service. Deleted right away when you withdraw your consent or delete your account; if you don't sign in for 12 months, deleted after a warning; from backups within 90 days. Details in Privacy Notice §6.

9. Your rights and how to use them

  • In the app: Profile › Privacy › Download my data · Withdraw my consent · Delete all my data · Coach memory.
  • By e-mail: support@elowaapp.com; we reply within 30 days.
  • Complaints: the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Board) or your data protection authority in the EU.

10. Children

Elowa is for people aged 18 and over; we do not knowingly process data of anyone under 18.

11. Country annexes

  • European Union and European Economic Area: the GDPR applies. The legal grounds under GDPR Art. 6 and Art. 9 are in Privacy Notice §4. You have the rights in GDPR Art. 15–22; you can complain to the supervisory authority in your country.
  • Germany: Elowa is not a medical device or a digital health application (DiGA); it is not prescribed and has no diagnostic or treatment purpose.
  • Türkiye: KVKK applies. The Turkish text of this policy prevails.

12. Changes

If there is an important change, we let you know in the app; if a change relies on your permission, we ask for your permission again. Earlier versions are available on request.

Elowa is a self-care app. It is not a substitute for therapy, medical diagnosis or treatment. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number.
