Elowa Privacy Notice

Version 1.0 · Effective: 26 October 2026

This notice is prepared under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Fulfilling the Obligation to Inform, and under Article 13 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The consent text is separate from this notice; reading this notice does not mean you agree to anything.

1. Data controller

the Elowa developer, an individual developer based in Türkiye who publishes the Elowa app. Contact: support@elowaapp.com. EU representative: not appointed yet.

3. How we collect it

Inside the app, when you enter it and while the app runs, electronically, by automatic or partly automatic means. If you link an account, from Apple or Google; we get your subscription status from Apple or Google through RevenueCat, our subscription service.

If you don't give permission: you can still use the app. In limited mode, the Hard moment button, support lines and general content are open; the personal plan, coach chat and mood log are off.

Personal plan and automated processing: your plan is prepared automatically from the topics you choose and your answers. The plan gives you suggestions; it does not produce a legal or similarly significant effect on you. Still, you can object to it and ask a person to look at it (KVKK Art. 11(1)(g); GDPR Art. 22): support@elowaapp.com.

Artificial intelligence: the coach is an AI, not a person. Its replies are produced by a language model (the provider is not chosen yet; once it is, it is named here).

6. How long we keep it

  • As long as you use the service.
  • If you withdraw your consent, your data about your emotional state is deleted right away.
  • If you delete your account, all your data is deleted right away.
  • If you don't sign in for 12 months, we first warn you through whatever channel we can reach you (app notification or e-mail); then we delete your data.
  • It is deleted from backups within 90 days at the latest.
  • Analytics data is kept for at most 14 months.
  • Billing and payment records are kept by Apple or Google for their legal retention period.

7. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 and GDPR Art. 15–22, you can:

  • learn whether your data is processed and ask for information about it,
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in line with that purpose,
  • know the recipients it is transferred to,
  • ask for incomplete or wrong data to be corrected,
  • ask for it to be deleted or destroyed, and for the recipients to be told about this,
  • object to an outcome against you that results solely from automated processing,
  • ask for compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing,
  • receive your data in a portable format (GDPR Art. 20),
  • object to processing and ask for processing to be restricted (GDPR Art. 18, Art. 21),
  • withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing does not make the processing before it unlawful.

How: you can do most of this yourself in the app: in Profile › Privacy you can download your data (PDF and JSON), withdraw your consent and delete all your data. For other requests, write to support@elowaapp.com in a way that lets us verify who you are. We reply free of charge within 30 days at the latest.

Complaints: you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Board) (kvkk.gov.tr). If you live in the EU, you can also contact the data protection authority in your country.

8. Age

Elowa is for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that you are under 18, we freeze the account and delete the data.

9. Changes

If this notice changes, we show it in the app. If the permission text changes, we ask for your permission again.

Elowa is a self-care app. It is not a substitute for therapy, medical diagnosis or treatment. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number.
