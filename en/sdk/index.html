Elowa Analytics and SDK Notice

Version 1.0 · Effective: 26 October 2026

Elowa is a mobile app; it does not use cookies. Instead, some pieces of software made by other companies (SDKs) run inside the app. This notice explains which ones run, what they collect and what you decide. Internal rule: docs/kvkk/event-allowlist.md.

1. Summary

SDK What for When it runs Can you turn it off
Google Firebase AnalyticsTo see, in aggregate, how the app is usedOnly if you tick the "usage measurement" box on the consent screenYes, Profile › Privacy › Usage measurement
Google Firebase CrashlyticsTo fix crashes and errorsAfter the first permission you give for the personal planIt stops if you withdraw the first permission
Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple PushTo deliver reminders and notificationsIf you allow notificationsYes, in your phone's settings or in Profile › Reminders

There is no advertising SDK. No advertising ID (IDFA, AAID) is collected; that's why the "allow tracking" (ATT) question doesn't appear on iOS.

2. What goes into analytics, and what doesn't

What goes in: neutral events such as "app opened", "onboarding finished", "plan created", "day 5 of the plan completed", "subscription started"; the app version, phone type, language, country, and a resettable installation ID generated by Firebase.

What never goes in: the topics you choose, your mood, your feelings, anything you write or say, whether you used Hard moment or the crisis screen, the safety screening, the coach's tone, your name, your e-mail, your location, your account ID.

3. Legal ground and transfers

  • Analytics: your separate explicit consent, off by default (KVKK Art. 5(1), Art. 6(2); GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). Not giving consent does not limit any feature of the app.
  • Crash logs: switched on based on the explicit consent you give for your personal plan to work; the logs do not contain what you write or sensitive data.
  • Google is a US company, and Firebase data may be processed in the US. The transfer relies on the standard contract under KVKK Art. 9 and the standard contractual clauses under the GDPR.
  • Retention: analytics data for at most 14 months, crash logs for at most 90 days.

4. Changing your decision

You can turn off analytics permission in Profile › Privacy whenever you want. When you turn it off, collection stops right away and we ask Google to delete the analytics data linked to this installation ID.

Elowa is a self-care app. It is not a substitute for therapy, medical diagnosis or treatment. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number.
