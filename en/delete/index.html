Elowa: delete your data

This page explains how to delete your account and your data in the Elowa app.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Elowa and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Privacy › Delete all my data.
  3. If you like, download your data as PDF and JSON first.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm and tap Delete for good.

Deletion starts right away. The app then opens fresh, like on the first day.

What is deleted

  • Your answers, your plan and the rituals you did
  • Your mood entries and what you wrote
  • Your chats with your coach and its memory
  • Your profile, settings, consent records and account ID
  • The safety plan and contacts on this phone

Server data is cleared from backups within 90 days at the latest.

What stays

  • Purchase records stay with the app store (Google Play or App Store); their rules apply.
  • Your subscription is not cancelled automatically. Cancel it in your Google Play or App Store account, before or after deleting your data.

If you removed the app

Removing the app from your phone does not delete your data on our servers. Send a deletion request to support@elowaapp.com with the subject "Data deletion request".

  • If you linked your account to Google or Apple, write from the linked e-mail address; we delete everything that belongs to that account.
  • If you used the app without an account, your data is not tied to an e-mail address and we may not be able to verify that it is yours. Write to us anyway and we will try to find a way together. If the app is still on your phone, deleting in the app always works.

We answer your request within 30 days at the latest.

Elowa is a self-care app. It is not a substitute for therapy, medical diagnosis or treatment. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number.
